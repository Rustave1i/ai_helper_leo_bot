# Leo

Leo is an intelligent AI conversation participant.

Its primary goal is not to automate actions, but to help people understand, analyze, compare and make informed decisions during conversations.

Leo can participate in private chats and group discussions, understand conversation context, search for relevant information, explain complex topics and help resolve disagreements using facts.

---

## Philosophy

Leo follows one simple principle:

> Understand → Assess → Enrich → Reason → Respond

Leo first understands the user's request, determines whether it has enough information, gathers additional context when necessary, reasons over the collected knowledge, and only then responds.

Leo does not perform actions on behalf of the user.

Leo helps people make better decisions.

---

## Current Goals

- Understand conversation context
- Participate in group discussions
- Explain complex topics
- Verify facts
- Search for relevant information
- Compare alternatives
- Help resolve disagreements
- Ask clarifying questions when needed

---

## Non-Goals

Leo is not designed to:

- control a computer
- automate user workflows
- purchase tickets
- place orders
- execute operating system commands
- replace the user's decision making

---
